# For recruiters

## Posting a job

A 6-section manual form: Role, Company, Compensation, Description, Skills, Match Settings. There is **no AI-writes-the-JD-for-you feature here** — that only exists on the [Admin](/features/admin#post-a-job) side. What recruiters do get is a "Neural Prediction" side panel: as you type the job summary, it analyzes what you've written (after a short pause) and shows an estimated match-pool size and the key skills it detected — it critiques/summarizes what you wrote, it doesn't draft it for you.

The **Auto-shortlist Threshold** slider (0–100%, default 75%) set here is the exact number [Auto-Pilot](/features/candidates#auto-pilot-auto-apply) compares candidate scores against for that job — set it low and more candidates auto-apply; set it high and fewer do. Jobs can be saved as a draft or activated, with a completion-percentage bar tracking how much of the form is filled in.

## Reviewing applicants

Each posted job shows its applicant count and status (active/closed/draft). Opening a job lists its applicants — **sort order depends on your plan**:

- **Starter (free)**: applicants are shown strictly in the order they applied (first-come-first-served). An amber banner says so explicitly, and the match-score display is locked behind a lock icon.
- **Pro (paid)**: applicants are ranked by AI match score instead.

Each applicant has a detail drawer (Profile / Resume tabs) with actions: **Shortlist** (moves them into the Talent Pipeline), **Pass/Reject**, **Message** (currently a stub — doesn't send anything yet), and **Undo/Restore**.

## Talent Pipeline

A drag-and-drop Kanban board with four stages: **Shortlisted → Interviewing → Offer Stage → Hired**. Cards show the match score, a preview of the candidate's skills, an "Auto" badge if they were auto-matched by Auto-Pilot rather than shortlisted manually, and — if the candidate has a phone number on file — a direct WhatsApp button with a prefilled message.

Moving a candidate here updates their view too: whatever stage you drag them to shows up immediately on the candidate's own [Applications page](/features/candidates#applications-tracker).

## Reports

Real-time analytics (recalculates instantly if you delete a job, no refresh needed):

- **KPI tiles**: Active Applicants, Offer Rate, Average Days Open, Open Mandates.
- **Hiring Funnel tab**: stage-by-stage conversion with drop-off percentage at each step.
- **Open Roles tab**: per-job applicant count and average match score.
- **Skill Demand tab**: a frequency chart of the skills required across all of your open roles.
- A sidebar breaks down Auto-Match vs. Manual sourcing, plus a live activity feed.

## Settings

Same tab layout as candidate settings, plus a **Company** tab (org name, website, bio, headcount). Billing compares Starter vs. Pro (₹1,999/mo) — see [Plans & pricing](/features/plans-pricing) for the full breakdown.
