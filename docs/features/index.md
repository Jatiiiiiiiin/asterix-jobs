# What is Asterix Jobs

This section documents **every feature in the product**, described the way a user experiences it — what they click, what they see, what actually happens behind the button. It's the opposite of the [Platform](/platform/overview) and [Reference](/reference/frontend) sections, which explain how the system is built; this explains what it *does*.

Asterix Jobs is a job-matching platform with two sides:

- **Candidates** — upload a resume, get an AI-computed match score against every open role, and either auto-apply hands-off or apply manually. See **[For candidates](/features/candidates)**.
- **Recruiters** — post roles, get candidates ranked by AI match score instead of sorting resumes by hand, and move them through a hiring pipeline. See **[For recruiters](/features/recruiters)**.

Plus a few things that sit outside that split:

- **[Campus Connect](/features/campus-connect)** — a college-gated, proctored AI-generated aptitude/technical test, used for bulk campus recruiting.
- **[Admin tools](/features/admin)** — internal moderation: job posting, blocked-user management, test results.
- **[Plans & pricing](/features/plans-pricing)** — what's free vs. what's paid, on both the candidate and recruiter side.
- **[Site-wide features](/features/platform-wide)** — signup, the landing page, the AI chat bubble, contact — things every user touches regardless of role.

## The one-sentence version of each headline feature

| Feature | What it actually does |
|---|---|
| AI Match Score | Every job gets a 0–100% score against your resume, with a highlight breakdown — not a black box, the score comes with *why*. |
| Auto-Pilot | When on, it **actually** auto-applies — no click — to any job that clears your (or the recruiter's) match threshold, on a recurring background check. |
| Ace Interview | One click generates a 3-part prep brief (Strengths / Gaps / Power Tips) for a specific job, from your resume + that JD. |
| AI Audit chat | A real per-job AI chat — ask "why did I score less" or "what should I learn" and get an answer scoped to that job and your resume. |
| Campus Connect | College students get a proctored, AI-generated 52-question test; results go straight to recruiters/admins, not back to the student. |
| Talent Pipeline | Recruiters drag candidates through Shortlisted → Interviewing → Offer → Hired; candidates see the same movement on their own Applications page in real time. |
